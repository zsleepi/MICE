Every actor has a species decided from their body.
your species will give you certain stat benefits and may change how NPCs treat you.
your species decides your character sprite.

how is species decided?

species is decided by counting up the species values of each body part, and then taking into consideration your body material and limb configuration.

you can have any even integer between 0 and 6 arms, and 0 and 8 legs
there are a variety of limb configurations but they tend to fall into a few categories.
- snake. no limbs.
- biped. 2 legs and any arm configuration.
- feral quadruped. 4+ legs and no arms.
- taur. 4+ legs and also having arms.

we'll probably just be working with these 4 types for categorization.
you can be just about any species with any limb configuration, though we cant make sprites for all of them, so instead focus on common cases.
sprite will default to generic species sprite if one cannot be found for that body configuration.

character sprite also depends on *visible* species, so you can wear clothes to hide your exact species.

bodymaterial modifies species type a little. any slime-material character gets slime species bonuses primarily. immaterial characters are ghosts.
