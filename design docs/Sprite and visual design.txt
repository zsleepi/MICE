POST PROCESSING
we can do post processing on overworld environments according to time of day and climate etc. I think this could really enhance mood through the use of color palette shifting and bloom effects and the like.

CHARACTER SPRITES
deciding a character's overworld sprite will probably end up coming down to a few variables.
species being the first and most important variable, is decided by a function that sums up the species values of each of a creature's body parts and outputs the highest fit, we can probably get creative with the criteria and implement hybrids and the like.
what is the second most important thing to communicate about a character? i'd say class, though im not sure if we'll have a class mechanic.
at the very least, merchants should be distinguished from regular civilians, and guards and casters get their own sprites, for maybe a total of 4 job sprites.
finally, we can have 3 variants of each, for a bit of extra randomness.

CHARACTER SPRITE COLORS
each character has a primary and secondary color that they're associated with. this will typically denote some kind of faction allegiance or family they belong to. for example a hive of insects will all share the same color scheme.
this could either be a very literal coloration or something more abstract.
it might just end up being that each character has an abstract set of 2 colors that they simply *are*, and these can be changed through tf or dyeing, or more interestingly it could be something more foundational about a character that isnt so easy to change. it really is just a question of what we think is a more interesting way of representing it diagetically.
we could call these sets of colors "signatures" for some spice :3
